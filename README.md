# Where AI Pays: find the AI projects that pay back first

A company answers a few questions (industry, what takes the team's time, rough volumes, costs) and gets its top AI use cases ranked by money saved, setup effort and risk, each with first steps, a link to a working example, and a one-page PDF plan.

No AI service and no stored data: the ranking is plain arithmetic in `finder.py` (time saved per unit × volume × the share AI realistically takes over × hourly cost, against setup and running costs), and every card shows its maths.
